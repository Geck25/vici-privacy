## Summary

Vici is a personal betting diary that runs entirely on your device. There are no
accounts, there are no servers, and we collect nothing: the data you enter stays
on your iPhone and is never transmitted to us or to third parties. We use no
analytics, we do not profile you, and we show no advertising. There is no
artificial intelligence of any kind, and no connection to bookmakers or gambling
operators.

## Who processes the data

Data controller: **Giacomo Muzzolon**
Contact: **geck.developer@gmail.com**

Because no data ever leaves your device, the controller has no access to the
information you enter in the app.

## What data the app handles

Vici stores on your device only what you enter yourself:

- **Bet slips and wagers**: stake, odds, outcome, date, optional notes.
- **Matches and selections** attached to a slip.
- **Cash movements**: deposits, withdrawals, starting balance.
- **Goals** you set.
- **App preferences**: light/dark theme, left-handed mode, slip zoom, onboarding
  state.

The app does **not** collect usage data, navigation analytics, crash logs,
advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or health data.

## Where it is stored

All data is saved in a local store inside the app's sandbox, on your device.
There is no synchronisation with iCloud or with any other service.

If you have **iCloud Backup** enabled (or you back your device up to a
computer), Vici's store is included in that backup, as is the case for most
apps. That backup is managed by Apple under Apple's own terms, is encrypted, and
is tied to your Apple ID: we have no access to it.

## Network connections

In the current version, Vici **makes no network connections at all**. The app
works entirely offline and sends no data to any server, including ours.

## Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to anyone, for any purpose.
No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are integrated.

## No connection to bookmakers or gambling operators

Vici is a record-keeping tool and nothing more. Specifically:

- **It is not connected to any bookmaker**, betting shop, casino, gaming
  network, or betting platform, in Italy or anywhere else.
- **It cannot place bets** and cannot be used to gamble: no function exists that
  sends a wager to an operator.
- **It handles no real money.** The amounts you see are numbers you typed
  yourself; the app has no access to gaming accounts, bank accounts, cards, or
  payment systems, and processes no transactions.
- **It shares no data with bookmakers or gambling operators**, and receives none
  from them. There is no channel, automatic or manual, through which your
  wagers could reach an operator.
- **It does not profile you and receives no compensation** for directing you to
  any operator: there are no affiliate links, promotional codes, banners, or
  betting suggestions.

The information in Vici describes wagers you have already placed elsewhere and
choose to write down, in the same way you would note them in a notebook.

## No artificial intelligence

Vici **uses no artificial intelligence whatsoever**. There are no AI models in
the app, no automated processing is performed on your data, and nothing you
enter — text, amounts, images of betting slips — is sent to third-party AI
services or used to train any model. The statistics and charts you see are plain
arithmetic computed on your device from the data you entered.

## Exporting and importing your data

From Settings you can export your data to a JSON file and import it back. The
file is created and read through the system file picker, in a location you
choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive, or another app). From that moment the
file is under your control, and this policy no longer governs where you keep it
or who you share it with.

Importing **replaces** the data currently in the app.

## Photos

When you share a picture of a betting slip, you can choose to save it to your
Photos library. In that case iOS asks your permission to **add** the image: Vici
does not read, browse, or otherwise access your existing photos.

## Notifications

Vici may ask permission to show a **badge** on the app icon with the number of
bets still open. That count is computed on your device. We send no remote push
notifications, and no server exists that could contact you.

## Tracking

Vici does not track users within the meaning of Apple's App Tracking
Transparency rules. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do
not link your data to information from other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

There are two ways to remove everything:

1. **Settings → Delete all data**, which permanently erases slips, matches, cash
   movements, and goals.
2. **Deleting the app**, which removes the entire local store.

Note that if you have an earlier iCloud backup, the data may still exist in that
backup until it is overwritten or deleted from your iCloud settings.

## Minors

Vici deals with betting and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Your rights

Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) grants you rights of access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability. Because Vici neither
transmits nor stores your data outside your device, the controller holds no
information against which to exercise those rights: you already have direct and
complete access to your data inside the app, you can correct it at any time,
export it in readable JSON, and delete it in full.

For any question you can still write to **geck.developer@gmail.com**.
